Who we are

GutCare Clinics is a specialist centre for digestive, colorectal and laparoscopic care, led by senior GI and colorectal surgeon Dr. Yuvraj Singh Gehlot.

We treat conditions many people find hard to talk about, such as piles, fissures and fistula, along with hernia, gallstones, appendicitis, acid reflux, constipation and colorectal cancer.

Wherever it is right for you, we use advanced laser and laparoscopic techniques, so treatment means less pain, smaller scars and a faster return to everyday life.
