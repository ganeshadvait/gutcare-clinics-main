FAQs

Questions patients often ask

Can't find your answer? Call us at +91 84315 50550.

Why should I choose GutCare Clinics?

GutCare offers expert digestive and colorectal care with experienced surgeons, accurate diagnosis and personalised treatment in a modern setting, using advanced laser and laparoscopic techniques.

What conditions do you treat?

We treat piles, anal fistula, anal fissures, hernia, gallstones, appendicitis, acid reflux/GERD, chronic constipation, colorectal cancer and other digestive conditions.

Is laser treatment for piles painful?

Laser procedures are minimally invasive and done under anaesthesia, so most patients feel little discomfort. Most patients are discharged the same day and resume routine activities within a few days.

How much does treatment cost?

We offer affordable consultation fees and transparent pricing. The final cost depends on your diagnosis and procedure. Call us at +91 84315 50550 for a personalised estimate.

Who leads the gastro care team?

The team is led by Dr. Yuvraj Singh Gehlot, a senior GI, laparoscopic and colorectal surgeon with 30 years in healthcare.
